## Proposed direction (AGENTS.md steps 1–3, for discussion, not built)

**Three sentences:**
- Kreore feels like a warm, feminine, faith-tinged luxury coffee experience carried from Colombia to New York by one founder.
- Visitors are event planners, offices and hotels looking for coffee service, plus Latina women in Queens and Brooklyn who want the community events.
- They should either **book Kreore for an event/office**, **order coffee**, or **join the next Kreore Woman gathering**.

**One idea: "varietal labels."** Each coffee line already has its own colour (Pink Bourbon pink, Gesha gold, Noir 82 black, Pacamara yellow, Pureza white). Treat the site like a set of coffee-bag labels: each section takes one varietal's colour as a full-bleed background, with label typography (origin, process, notes) as the structural device. The motto *Creo porque oré* is the hero line.

**Homepage sketch:**
1. Hero on black: a huge serif "KREORE" with *Creo porque oré* and the Colombia ↔ NYC line. One big farm photo, colour-graded.
2. Pink Bourbon (pink): Viviana's story in her own words, with a portrait.
3. The coffees: label cards in varietal colours, varied sizes rather than an equal grid. Prices only once the client confirms them.
4. Gold: services for offices, catering, hotels and pop-ups. Primary CTA is the "Host your event" form or WhatsApp.
5. Blush: the Kreore Woman community, with event photos and a "next gathering" slot that stays empty unless confirmed.
6. Black footer: MoCADA mention (only if confirmed), contacts, and an ES/EN toggle.
